# Privacy Policy – My Daily Book

Last Updated: June 2026

My Daily Book ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the My Daily Book mobile application.

## Information We Collect

When you use My Daily Book, we may collect and store the following information:

* Name
* Phone Number
* Email Address
* Customer transaction records
* Cash, payment, and credit (udhar) records entered by you

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

* Create and manage your account
* Store and synchronize your business records
* Provide customer management features
* Generate reports and transaction history
* Improve app performance and reliability
* Provide customer support when needed

## Authentication

My Daily Book uses secure authentication services provided through Supabase to manage user accounts and login functionality.

## Data Storage

Your data is securely stored in cloud databases managed through Supabase infrastructure. We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or modification.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Your information is not shared with third parties except:

* When required by law
* To comply with legal obligations
* To provide essential app infrastructure and services

## Data Security

We take reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your Rights

You may:

* Access your account information
* Update your information
* Delete records within the application
* Request account deletion by contacting us

## Children's Privacy

My Daily Book is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
